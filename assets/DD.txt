When an external person enters a computer system without being authorized to do so. This offence is better known as hacking. The violation of access authorization by an “insider” is also considered hacking. Simply entering a computer system without authorization is punishable, even if the intruder only wants to test whether they can access it. Intrusion with the aim of identifying security vulnerabilities is also punishable, unless prior authorization has been obtained from the owner of the computer system (“ethical hacking”). As soon as data is actually modified, deleted, added, copied, or damaged, a more severe sanction will be applied. This aggravation applies even if the damage to the data was not intentional. An attempt to enter a computer system will be punished as severely as actually obtaining access. Anyone who orders the hacking and/or anyone who uses data that they know was obtained through hacking is also punishable by law (Rombaut, 2022).
